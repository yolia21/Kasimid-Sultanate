Official Bulletins

AUG 05, 2025

Diplomatic Relations Established

The Sultanate reinforces its commitment to the Union of North Atlantic Micronations (UNAM) and the Organization of Islamic Micronations (OIM).

JUL 27, 2025

Constitution Ratified

His Majesty Sultan Yusuf I has officially signed the 2025 Constitution, establishing the rights of citizens and the structure of the Majlis.

JUL 25, 2025

Declaration of Independence

The Kasimid Sultanate is officially founded following the dissolution of the Raritan Caliphate.
