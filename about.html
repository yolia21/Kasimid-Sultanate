Our History & Heritage

From the early Lenape inhabitants to the establishment of the Sultanate in 2025.

The Story of the Nation

Etymology

The name "Kasimid" is derived from the dynasty’s ancestor, Kasim al-Raniri, a semi-nomadic trader from the Maldives. The term refers to the dynasty established in his name and serves as the basis for our national identity.

Early Inhabitants

Long before modern development, the region was home to the Lenape tribes, specifically the Raritan and Navesink groups. The first modern settlement in Ghabaani Province was established in 1988.

The Founding (2025)

On July 25, 2025, following the dissolution of the Raritan Caliphate, Sultan Yusuf I established the Kasimid Sultanate. A new constitution was ratified to ensure stability and Islamic governance.

Culture & Customs

A Blend of Tradition

The culture of the Kasimid Sultanate is a unique fusion of maritime trade heritage and rugged highland traditions. We emphasize family, religious observance, and strong social cohesion.


National Holidays

  • Eid al-Fitr: Marking the end of Ramadan.
  • Festival of Fragrance: A spring celebration of nature.
  • Date Harvest Festival: Celebrating the summer bounty.
